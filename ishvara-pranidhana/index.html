Yama & Niyama

Niyama · The Tenth Observance · 10

ईश्वर प्रणिधान

Iishvara Prańidhána

Shelter in the Cosmic

To take the whole of the infinite as your one ideal, and move toward it with accelerated speed. The last of the ten — and the greatest.

Scroll

The word

Who you take shelter in, and how.

ईश्वर

Iishvara

The controller of this universe — He who governs its thought-waves. The witnessing entity of the causal world, free from all bondage. To a sádhaka, nothing other than God.

प्रणिधान

Prańidhána

To understand clearly, and to adopt something as a shelter. So Iishvara prańidhána means to establish yourself in the Cosmic idea — to accept Iishvara as the only ideal of life.

The body never disobeys the laws of His thought-cycle. It is the mind that violates them — and the mind that, here, learns to come home.

An effort of feeling

Your Iishvara is not deaf.

This is no outward show. Shouting for a crowd to gather, beating drums to prove your devotion — none of it has any place here. Respect, affection, sincerity, devotion: these belong to the inner heart, not the loud language of flatterers. Iishvara prańidhána is based entirely on bháva, on ideation. It is a mental effort in its entirety. Don't shout to convey your feelings to Him.

The definition

Klesha-karma-vipákáshaer​aparámrśt́ah Puruśavisheśa Iishvarah.

He is the subtlest Entity — the Supreme Consciousness untouched by affliction, action, or its fruits. Withdraw the mind from the limited “I”, gather it to a point, then take the thought of the Macrocosm around that point. He can be realized only through feeling, and by no other means.

Three ways to call

The lowest

वाचनिक

Vácanika

Spoken aloud, to attract His attention by reciting prayers in a loud voice. Absolutely meaningless — it serves no purpose at all.

What it opens

The mind moves faster than its Maker.

Practised regularly and properly, mental japa carries the mind forward along the path of Pratisaiṋcara. The speed a sádhaka generates this way is faster than the speed by which Brahma leads all creation toward perfection. The animative force, divinity's own potential, rises above every tendency, every saḿskára — and proceeds toward eternal bliss. Pay no attention to the outer signs along the way. Cosmic feeling is above everything else.

The practice

Practise it alone. And do not miss it together.

In a lonely place

Alone

Like every lesson of sádhaná, Iishvara prańidhána is to be practised by yourself, in solitude. The mind withdraws, gathers, and ideates — witnessed by nothing but itself.

Dharmacakra

Together

Unlike the others, this one may also be done collectively. Combined mental efforts work as one and raise the higher signs in a very short time — an indomitable force that helps solve any problem on earth.

So be zealous to meet for weekly dharmacakra. What one mind cannot, many ideating as one can.

The last observance

Of all ten, this is the most important.

The nine before it cleared the ground — conduct toward the world, conduct toward the self, the mind washed and steadied and turned to understand. Iishvara Prańidhána is where they were always headed: shelter in the Cosmic, held in higher esteem than the rest. The whole guide ends not with a rule but with an invitation — to move, with accelerated speed, toward the one Shelter that does not end.
